HOW TO PLAY

Cameras – Onboard, Rear cam (locked behind the car) or TV: trackside cameras pan and zoom to follow you and cut to the next camera as you go round. Controls are always relative to the car.
ERS – hold boost for extra power; it recharges when you brake.
DRS – from lap 2, cross the detection line less than 1 s behind and the rear wing opens on the next straight. In single player it opens in every DRS zone.
Slipstream – sit right behind your rival for a tow.
Hot lap (1 player) – rolling start, endless timed laps, live delta, ERS refilled at the line. Leave the track in the last sector and the next lap is deleted too. R restarts the run. T shows the lap telemetry.
🎈 Battle – no track: a round arena where everyone has 3 balloons. Items from the ? boxes pop them – oil, a loose tyre (it bounces off the walls), a brick wall, or ramming someone with your turbo on. Lose all three and you're out; last car with balloons wins (or the most after 3 minutes).
📅 Daily (1 player) – one track and one condition a day (rain, worn tyres, no ERS, no DRS, full tanks, storm or glitter), the same for everyone, as a hot lap with its own leaderboard: the "📅 Today" tab in Records. A new one every day at 00:00 UTC.
Ghost – in Hot lap, chase a see-through replay of the track record (gold) or your own best lap (blue). It starts at the line; G switches it.
Track limits – all four wheels past the white line (kerbs are outside it) deletes that lap time. In races: go wide and keep your speed on the kerb or run-off → warning; cut a corner → slow down to give the time back + warning; a real mistake (grass, big moment) isn't punished again. After 3 warnings each one costs +5 s.
🌐 Online – one of you creates a room and shares the 4-letter code (or the invite link); everyone joins from their own browser and the host picks the track, Race or Hot lap together, and starts it for everyone. Each car is driven on its own computer and the others appear live (a fraction of a second behind). No contact between cars yet; pausing doesn't stop an online race.
Records – set your name, drive a valid lap, and your personal best goes on the online leaderboard ("Player 1/2" isn't uploaded).
CPU drivers – add 1, 3 or 5 computer opponents (Easy / Medium / Hard) to a race or Chaos. They race hard but fair, pass slower cars and use items too; their laps don't count for records. 🏆 Cup (next to the tracks): all six tracks in a row, points 10-8-6-5-4-3-2.
Stunt tracks – short time-attack circuits: full-width boost pads, big jump ramps, ice and dirt patches, hills and two checkpoints with your split against your best lap. Beat the medal times (🏁 Author, 🥇 Gold, 🥈 Silver, 🥉 Bronze) shown on the menu; Hot lap is the time attack, R restarts.
Chaos – mystery boxes give you Turbo Brick, Oil Slick, Loose Tyre, Safety Car (slows the leader), Local Shower (rain on the car ahead), Phantom (drive through anything), Brick Wall (drop behind) or Pit Stop (fixes you). The boost key fires it; whoever is behind gets better items. Blue pads boost you, yellow ramps launch you. Drift: hold throttle + brake while steering – blue sparks after 1 s, orange after 2 s – and let go for a boost. The weather changes every lap.
Keys – Esc pause · M mute · T telemetry · V replay (results/pause: TV, onboard, rear or free camera, 0.25×–4×) · P photo mode · gamepads work too.
