TV camera – each player gets their own broadcast director: trackside cameras pan and zoom to follow your car and cut to the next camera as you go round. Controls are always relative to the car. ERS – hold boost for extra power; recharges when you brake. DRS – from lap 2, cross the detection line less than 1 s behind and the rear wing opens on the next straight. Slipstream – sit right behind your rival for a tow. Handling – Arcade is forgiving; F1 has more grip and braking in slow corners and lets you over-rotate the car (and spin it). Records are kept separately. Track limits – all four wheels past the kerb deletes that lap; after 3 warnings each one costs +5 s. Cut a corner or chicane and you must slow down to give the time back. Grass is slow; cut the infield and you're put back on track.
