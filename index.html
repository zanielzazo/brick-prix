HOW TO PLAY

Cameras – Onboard, Rear cam (locked behind the car) or TV: trackside cameras pan and zoom to follow you and cut to the next camera as you go round. Controls are always relative to the car.
ERS – hold boost for extra power; it recharges when you brake.
DRS – from lap 2, cross the detection line less than 1 s behind and the rear wing opens on the next straight. In single player it opens in every DRS zone.
Slipstream – sit right behind your rival for a tow.
Hot lap (1 player) – rolling start, endless timed laps, live delta. R restarts the run.
Ghost – in Hot lap, chase a see-through replay of the track record (gold) or your own best lap (blue). It starts at the line; G switches it.
Handling – F1 has more grip and braking in slow corners and lets you over-rotate the car (and spin it); Arcade is forgiving. Records are kept separately.
Track limits – all four wheels past the white line (kerbs are outside it) deletes that lap time. In races: go wide and keep your speed on the kerb or run-off → warning; cut a corner → slow down to give the time back + warning; a real mistake (grass, big moment) isn't punished again. After 3 warnings each one costs +5 s.
Records – set your name, drive a valid lap, and your personal best goes on the online leaderboard ("Player 1/2" isn't uploaded).
CPU drivers – add 1, 3 or 5 computer opponents (Easy / Medium / Hard) to a race or Chaos. They race hard but fair, pass slower cars and use items too; their laps don't count for records. 🏆 Cup (next to the tracks): all five tracks in a row, points 10-8-6-5-4-3-2.
Chaos – mystery boxes give you Turbo Brick, Oil Slick, Loose Tyre, Safety Car (slows the leader), Local Shower (rain on the car ahead), Phantom (drive through anything), Brick Wall (drop behind) or Pit Stop (fixes you). The boost key fires it; whoever is behind gets better items. Blue pads boost you, yellow ramps launch you. Drift: hold throttle + brake while steering – blue sparks after 1 s, orange after 2 s – and let go for a boost. The weather changes every lap.
Keys – Esc pause · M mute · gamepads work too (triggers, stick, Y camera).
