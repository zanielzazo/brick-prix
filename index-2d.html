ERS – hold boost for extra power; the battery recharges when you brake. DRS – from lap 2, if you cross the detection line (Turn 15) less than 1 s behind, the rear wing opens on the main straight. Braking closes it. Slipstream – sit right behind your rival to get a tow. Kerbs are fine, run-off is slow, grass is very slow. Cut across the infield and you get put back on track.
